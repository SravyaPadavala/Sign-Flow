Algorithmic Innovations: How SignFlow++ Outperforms the Base Paper

Comparison of architectural mechanisms and theoretical principles that eliminate errors.

1

True Dual-Stream Cross-Attention

The base paper extracted MediaPipe Pose features only for pre-training and discarded them during inference. SignFlow++ performs real-time bidirectional cross-attention: the RGB CNN features query exact 3D skeletal velocities, eliminating finger shape ambiguities.

2

Visual Alignment Constraint (VAC)

Pure CTC gradients passing backwards through 8 Transformer layers suffer from severe vanishing gradients in the 3D-CNN. Our auxiliary visual & pose CTC heads plus the VAC symmetric KL loss force the CNN to learn gloss-discriminative visual boundaries directly.

3

Relative Conformer & MSTC

Instead of choosing between no position encoding (which loses word order) or absolute position encoding (which hurts keyword detection), Conformer combines depthwise temporal convolutions with relative positional self-attention, seamlessly handling rapid signing.
